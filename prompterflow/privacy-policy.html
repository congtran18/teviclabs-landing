Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Summary

PrompterFlow records and processes everything on your device. We do not collect, upload or store your videos, audio, scripts or speech. The app has no ads, no analytics SDKs and no account.

2. How the App Uses Your Data

  • Scripts: Scripts you write or import are saved only inside the app on your device.
  • Videos: Recordings are written to your device and saved to your Photos library only when you tap Save. You decide where to share them.
  • Voice-Follow: To scroll the script with your voice, the app uses Apple's Speech framework. When your device supports on-device recognition for the selected language, speech is processed on the device. Otherwise iOS may send audio to Apple to perform the recognition, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive this audio or its transcript.

3. Permissions We Request

  • Camera and Microphone: Used to record your video and audio.
  • Speech Recognition: Used only for Voice-Follow, while it is turned on.
  • Photos (add only): Used to save the recordings you choose to keep. The app cannot read your existing photos.
  • Notifications (optional): If you start a free trial, we ask to remind you the day before it ends.

4. App Settings File

When you open the app, it downloads a small public settings file from our hosting provider (for example, the length of free recordings and the address of this policy). The request contains no personal data, although the hosting provider receives your IP address as part of any internet connection. The file only changes how the app behaves; nothing about you is sent.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

6. Children

PrompterFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@teviclabs.com.
