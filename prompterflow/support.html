Frequently Asked Questions

How does Voice-Follow AI work?

PrompterFlow uses Apple's on-device Speech framework. As you speak, the prompter highlights your spoken words in real time and automatically advances the script. If you pause to take a breath or improvise, the prompter pauses with you.

How do I maintain 100% eye contact?

The teleprompter text box is positioned directly underneath your iPhone front camera lens. By looking directly at the text near the top of your screen, your viewers perceive natural, direct eye contact throughout the recording.

How do I restore my PRO purchase?

If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, open PrompterFlow, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your iOS device settings: Go to iPhone Settings > [Your Apple ID] > Subscriptions > PrompterFlow.

Where are my recordings saved?

Recorded videos can be saved directly to your iPhone Photos library or managed in the PrompterFlow internal Recordings Vault for privacy and peace of mind.
